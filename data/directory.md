## Escalation

If a question does not clearly belong to one team:

1. Contact People Ops at people-ops@example.com.
2. People Ops will route the question to the appropriate team.

For urgent security or compliance concerns, contact Security & Compliance at
security@example.com directly.

For missing or damaged equipment, contact IT & Equipment at
it-onboarding@example.com.
