# Offer Packet — Anna Müller, Software Engineer

## Start date and first day
Your start date is confirmed in your offer letter, and the app shows the countdown
from there — this packet won't repeat the date since it can go stale. Arrive at the
Wolfsburg office by 9:00 AM and check in at reception; ask for Sarah Weber, your
manager, who will walk you to your desk. Bring a government-issued photo ID —
verification of your right to work cannot be completed without it.

## Before your first day checklist
Bring a government-issued photo ID for right-to-work verification. No action is
needed on your reference check unless People Operations contacts you directly — it's
already in progress. Watch for your laptop; it should arrive about a week before your
start date, and if it hasn't by the Wednesday before, contact
workplace-it@dayone.demo. There's nothing to complete for benefits before day one —
enrollment opens on your start date, not before.

## Equipment
IT ships your laptop (a company-standard notebook) to your home address about one
week before your start date. If it hasn't arrived by the Wednesday before you start,
contact workplace-it@dayone.demo. A monitor, keyboard, and desk setup are waiting at
your assigned desk in the Wolfsburg office.

## Benefits enrollment
Company benefits enrollment (supplemental health, meal allowance, and the company
pension top-up) opens on your start date and stays open for 30 days. Statutory health
insurance is arranged separately through your chosen provider before day one — your
offer letter has the details People Operations needs from you.

## Compensation and signing bonus
Your signing bonus (as stated in your offer letter) pays out with your second
paycheck, not your first. It is subject to a 12-month clawback if you leave
voluntarily before your first anniversary.

## Reference check
Your offer is contingent on a clean reference check, which is already in progress.
You do not need to do anything unless People Operations contacts you directly.

## Vacation days
Your statutory vacation entitlement is set out in your offer letter and is visible in
the HR system once your account is provisioned, typically by your second week.
