# Family and Dependents

## What parental leave am I entitled to?

Parental leave combines statutory entitlements (which vary by country) with additional company-provided leave on top. In most locations, the primary caregiver receives extended paid leave (commonly 12-16 weeks depending on country), and the secondary caregiver receives a shorter paid leave (commonly 2-4 weeks). Exact entitlements for your specific country are detailed in the country-specific parental leave policy in the HR portal — reach out to Benefits or HR to get your precise numbers, since statutory minimums differ substantially by location.

## How do I notify the company about an upcoming birth or adoption?

Notify your manager and HR as early as you're comfortable, ideally at least 2 months before your expected leave start date, so a coverage plan can be arranged for your role. HR will walk you through the parental leave request process, required documentation, and how it coordinates with any statutory leave programs in your country.

## Is childcare support available?

Some offices offer either an on-site childcare partnership, a childcare subsidy, or a dependent care flexible spending account (FSA) that lets you set aside pre-tax income for childcare costs — availability depends on your office location. Check the Family and Dependents section of the benefits portal for what applies at your specific location.

## Can I add my children to my health insurance?

Yes, dependents (spouse, domestic partner, and children) can be added to your health plan during your initial 30-day enrollment window, or afterward during a qualifying life event like birth or adoption. See the Healthcare and Insurance document for the enrollment process and required documentation.

## What if I have a family emergency during my first weeks?

Family emergencies are handled with flexibility, especially during onboarding — talk to your manager directly and as soon as you're able. Sick leave, emergency personal days, or in serious cases short-term personal leave can typically be arranged quickly; the standard notice periods for planned time off don't apply to genuine emergencies.

## Does the company offer bereavement leave?

Yes, paid bereavement leave is available for the loss of an immediate family member, typically 3-5 days depending on your location's policy, with the option to request additional unpaid leave if needed. Contact HR to arrange this — it doesn't need to go through the standard vacation request process.

## Is there support for elder care or dependent adults?

Some benefit plans include access to elder care referral services and consultations through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), available to all employees regardless of health plan enrollment. Check the EAP section of the benefits portal, or reach out to the Benefits Team for what's specifically available in your location.

## Can my partner or spouse be added even if we're not legally married?

Yes, domestic partners can typically be added to your benefits, though the specific documentation required (such as a domestic partnership affidavit) varies by country and sometimes by state/region. Check with the Benefits Team on the exact documentation needed for your situation before the enrollment window closes.

## What if my family situation changes after the 30-day enrollment window?

Qualifying life events — marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage — open a special enrollment window (typically 30 days from the event) allowing you to adjust your benefits outside the normal annual cycle. Report the life event through the benefits portal as soon as it happens to trigger this window.
