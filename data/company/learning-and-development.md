# Learning and Development

## Is there a budget for courses or certifications?

Yes, most roles have an annual learning and development budget (amount varies by level and role, detailed in your offer letter or the L&D policy in the HR portal) that can be used for courses, certifications, books, or conference tickets relevant to your role. Requests over a certain threshold typically need manager pre-approval.

## What internal learning resources are available?

The company maintains an internal course catalog covering both role-specific skills and general professional development (communication, leadership, tools training), accessible through the Learning portal once your accounts are active. Some courses are self-paced; others run on a cohort schedule with live sessions.

## Can I attend external conferences?

Yes, subject to budget availability and manager approval — submit a request through the L&D portal including the conference details and expected cost, ideally at least a month in advance for budget planning purposes. Conference costs count against your annual L&D budget unless your team has separate conference funding.

## Is there a mentorship program?

Many teams offer informal or structured mentorship pairing beyond the standard onboarding buddy — check with your manager about what's available for your specific team or department, since this varies more by team than by company-wide policy.

## When do performance and development conversations happen?

Beyond your onboarding check-ins in the first weeks, most teams have regular 1:1s with your manager (frequency varies by team, commonly weekly or biweekly) where development goals and growth areas are discussed on an ongoing basis, separate from the formal annual/biannual performance review cycle.

## Are there opportunities to switch teams or roles internally?

Yes, internal mobility is generally supported after a minimum tenure in your current role (commonly 12 months, to ensure a fair ramp-up period, though this can sometimes be discussed earlier with manager and HR agreement in special circumstances). Internal openings are posted on the internal careers portal before or alongside external posting.

## What happens during the new hire welcome session?

A New Hire Welcome session runs on a regular cadence (commonly every other Friday) covering company overview, culture, and a chance to meet other recent hires across teams — you'll receive a calendar invite automatically during your first week.

## Does the company pay for a professional certification exam?

Certification exam costs relevant to your role are generally covered under your L&D budget, subject to the same approval process as courses. If the certification is a requirement of your role (rather than optional development), check with your manager, since role-required certifications sometimes have separate budget handling outside your personal L&D allowance.

## Is there language training available?

Some offices offer language courses (e.g. for employees working in a country where their primary language isn't the local one), typically subsidized similarly to other L&D resources. Availability varies significantly by office location — check with local HR or the L&D portal for what's offered at your specific office.
