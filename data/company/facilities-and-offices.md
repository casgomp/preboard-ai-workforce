# Facilities and Offices

## Where do I go on my first day?

Report to the main reception on the ground floor of your assigned office. Someone from HR will meet you there at 9:00 AM — if you're running late or can't find the building, call the number listed in your Day One welcome packet and someone will come let you in.

## Do I get a parking space?

Parking is available on a first-come basis for office-based roles, subject to availability at your specific office location. Request a spot through the Facilities request form at least one week before your start date — spots are not guaranteed and are allocated on a rolling basis, so requesting early matters. If no spot is available, most offices are near public transit and the company offers a commuter benefit to offset costs.

## Is there a dress code?

Business casual is the general standard for office days. Some teams (particularly engineering and creative roles) are more casual day-to-day — your manager will confirm specifics for your team in your welcome email. Client-facing roles or days with external meetings may require more formal attire, which your manager will flag in advance.

## How do I get my building access badge?

Your badge is issued on your first day at the reception desk during your morning check-in with HR — bring a government-issued photo ID to have it activated. The badge grants access to your office floor and common areas; if your role requires access to restricted areas (server rooms, executive floors), that's provisioned separately and may take a day or two after your badge is issued.

## Is there a gym in the building?

This varies by office location — some larger offices have an on-site gym or partner with a nearby fitness facility at a discounted rate for employees. Check the office-specific facilities guide (linked in your welcome email) for what's available at your location.

## Can I book a desk or meeting room?

Most offices use a hybrid desk-booking system rather than fully fixed desks — you can reserve a desk for the day through the facilities app, available once your accounts are set up. Meeting rooms are booked directly through your calendar app; room availability shows up automatically when scheduling a meeting.

## How does mail and package delivery work at the office?

Personal packages can generally be delivered to the office mailroom under your name; pickup is at the front desk or mailroom depending on office size. This is a convenience, not a guarantee — high-value items are better sent to your home address. Business mail addressed to you is sorted and delivered to your desk or team area.

## What do I do if I need to work from a different office temporarily?

Most offices support drop-in visits from employees based elsewhere — bring your badge, which typically works across company locations, and check in at reception on arrival. If you're planning an extended stay at another office, let your manager and the local office's Facilities contact know in advance so a desk can be arranged.

## What if I forget my badge or lock myself out?

Reception can issue a temporary day badge after verifying your identity — this happens often enough that it's a completely normal request, not something to feel awkward about. If it happens repeatedly, Facilities may follow up to make sure your badge issue is fixed permanently.

## Are there quiet rooms or spaces for calls?

Yes, most offices have designated phone booths or quiet rooms for calls and focused work, bookable the same way as meeting rooms through the facilities app for larger reservations, or first-come-first-served for single-person phone booths.
