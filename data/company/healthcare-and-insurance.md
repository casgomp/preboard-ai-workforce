# Healthcare and Insurance

## When does my health insurance start?

Health insurance coverage begins on your official start date for most plans. Some providers apply a short administrative waiting period of up to 5 business days before your coverage becomes active in their system, but you are covered retroactively to your start date regardless. You'll receive a welcome email from our benefits provider within the first week with your member ID and digital insurance card.

## How do I enroll my spouse or dependents?

You can add a spouse, partner, or children to your health plan during the open enrollment window, which is open for your first 30 days of employment. Log into the benefits portal (link sent in your Week 1 welcome email) and select "Add Dependent." You'll need each dependent's full legal name, date of birth, and Social Security number or equivalent national ID. Enrollment outside this 30-day window is only possible during the annual open enrollment period or after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption).

## What health plans are offered?

We offer three tiers: a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) paired with a company-funded Health Savings Account, a standard PPO plan, and a premium PPO plan with lower out-of-pocket costs. Dental and vision coverage are separate opt-in plans, both heavily subsidized by the company. Full plan comparison sheets, including premiums and coverage details, are available in the benefits portal once your account is activated.

## Is there a waiting period before I can use my insurance?

For most routine care, no — your coverage is active from your start date even if your physical insurance card hasn't arrived yet. For certain elective procedures, some plans require a 90-day waiting period; this is called out explicitly in the plan comparison sheet for any plan where it applies. If you need care during your first week and haven't received your member ID yet, contact the Benefits Team directly and they can provide temporary proof of coverage.

## Who do I contact with benefits questions?

For any question not covered here — plan comparisons, cost estimates, adding a dependent outside the enrollment window, or a claims issue — reach out to the Benefits Team at benefits@company.com. They typically respond within one business day.
