# Travel and Expenses

## How do I book business travel?

Business travel is booked through the company's travel portal (link and access provisioned once you're active in the system, typically your first week), which handles flights, hotels, and rental cars within pre-approved policy limits. For anything outside standard policy limits, manager approval is required before booking.

## How do I get reimbursed for expenses?

Submit expenses through the expense management tool with itemized receipts attached — this is available once your accounts are set up. Reimbursements are typically processed within the next 1-2 payroll cycles after approval. Keep digital or physical receipts for anything over a minimal threshold (specifics in the expense policy document), since expenses without receipts may be rejected or require additional justification.

## What's covered under the expense policy?

Standard reimbursable categories include business travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation), client meals within a per-person limit, work-related supplies not provided by IT/Facilities, and professional development costs approved in advance. The full expense policy document, including specific dollar limits by category, is in the Finance section of the HR portal.

## Is there a per diem for meals during travel?

Yes, business travel includes a daily meal per diem (amount varies by destination cost of living, detailed in the travel policy) rather than requiring itemized meal receipts for every meal during a trip — though you should still keep a general record in case of an audit.

## Do I need approval before booking international travel?

Yes, any international business travel requires manager approval in advance, both for budget reasons and because some destinations may have visa, safety, or company travel-restriction considerations that need to be checked first. Submit the request through the travel portal, which will flag anything requiring additional review.

## What if my flight is delayed or cancelled during business travel?

Contact the travel provider's 24/7 support line (included in your booking confirmation) for rebooking assistance first, since this is usually fastest. If it affects your ability to attend the reason for travel, also let your manager know as soon as possible so plans can adjust.

## Can I extend a business trip for personal travel?

Yes, this is generally allowed ("bleisure" travel) as long as you clearly separate personal costs (additional hotel nights, changed flight costs beyond the business-required itinerary) from business expenses in your expense report, and any personal extension doesn't need separate approval beyond informing your manager of your travel dates.

## What if I don't have a corporate card yet and need to travel soon?

New hires may not have a corporate card issued immediately — if travel comes up before yours arrives, you can pay out of pocket and submit for reimbursement, or ask your manager about a temporary advance for larger expenses. Corporate card requests can be expedited through Finance if travel is imminent.

## Are commuting costs covered?

Regular daily commuting to your assigned office is generally not reimbursed, consistent with standard practice, though some office locations offer a commuter benefit (public transit subsidy or parking assistance) — check the Facilities and Offices document or your local office's benefits summary for what applies at your location.

## Who do I contact with expense or travel questions?

For travel booking issues, use the support line included in your travel portal confirmation. For expense policy questions or reimbursement delays, contact finance@company.com.
