# IT and Security

## When will I receive my laptop?

Your laptop ships or is ready for office pickup 5 business days before your start date. You'll receive a shipping confirmation with tracking, or pickup instructions if you're based at an office location, via the personal email you provided during onboarding. If you haven't heard anything 3 business days before your start date, contact the IT Helpdesk immediately so there's time to resolve it before day one.

## Can I use my own laptop instead of a company one?

Bring-your-own-device (BYOD) is supported for some roles, primarily non-engineering positions that don't require access to sensitive internal systems. If you'd like to use your own device, contact IT before your start date to confirm your role is eligible. Approved BYOD setups require installing our mobile device management (MDM) profile and endpoint security software before you can access company email or internal tools.

## How do I set up my company email and accounts?

You'll receive your login credentials via your personal email approximately 3 days before your start date, along with a step-by-step setup guide. This covers your company email, single sign-on (SSO) profile, and initial access to core tools like Slack and the HR portal. Role-specific system access (e.g. Jira, GitHub, Salesforce) is provisioned automatically starting on your first day; if something is still missing after day one, submit a ticket to IT.

## What if my laptop or account setup doesn't work on day one?

This happens occasionally and isn't something to worry about — IT staffs extra support during new-hire weeks specifically for this. Report to the IT Helpdesk desk (location will be in your Day One welcome packet) or message the #it-helpdesk Slack channel once you have access, and someone will help you get unblocked, usually within the hour.

## What security software is required on my device?

All company-issued and approved BYOD devices must run our endpoint security agent, which handles disk encryption, malware protection, and compliance monitoring. This is installed automatically on company laptops before they're shipped. For BYOD, IT will walk you through installation during your pre-start IT setup call. You're also required to enable two-factor authentication on your company accounts within your first week — you'll get a prompt to set this up the first time you log in.

## What equipment comes with my laptop?

Standard equipment for office-based roles includes the laptop, a charger, and a laptop bag. Remote and hybrid employees additionally receive a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and webcam shipped to their home address. If your role requires specialized equipment (a second monitor, a graphics tablet, a headset for calls), request it through the IT equipment request form — most standard requests are approved automatically.

## What operating system and software do I get?

You'll be assigned either a Windows or Mac laptop depending on your role and team standard (engineering roles default to Mac; most other roles default to Windows, but exceptions can be requested). Core software — email client, browser, Slack, video conferencing, and any role-specific tools — comes pre-installed. Additional software requests go through the IT self-service portal; most common business software is available for instant install, while anything requiring a paid license may need manager approval.

## Can I access company systems from my personal phone?

Yes, for email, Slack, and calendar — install the company's MDM-managed apps from your phone's app store and register your device during first login. This requires accepting the mobile device policy, which allows the company to enforce a passcode and remotely wipe company data (not your personal data) if the device is lost or stolen. Access to more sensitive internal systems from mobile is restricted by role.

## What do I do if I lose my laptop or think my account was compromised?

Contact the IT Helpdesk immediately, any time of day — this is one of the few things with a 24/7 emergency contact, listed in your Day One packet and pinned in the #it-helpdesk Slack channel. Don't wait for business hours. IT can remotely lock or wipe the device and reset your credentials immediately to limit exposure.

## How do I request access to a system I don't have?

Submit a request through the IT self-service portal, specifying the system and a brief reason. Most requests route automatically to your manager for approval, then are provisioned within one business day. If it's urgent, mention it in the #it-helpdesk Slack channel and reference your ticket number.

## What's the WiFi password for the office?

Office WiFi uses your company SSO login rather than a shared password, for security reasons — connect to the network named "Company-Secure" and authenticate with your company email and password. Guest WiFi (for visitors) uses a rotating password available at the front desk. If you're having trouble connecting on your first day, IT support at the reception desk can help you get set up.

## Do I need a VPN to work remotely?

Yes, for accessing certain internal systems and file servers — the VPN client comes pre-installed on your company laptop and connects automatically via SSO. General tools like email, Slack, and most cloud-based software don't require the VPN. IT will let you know during setup if your specific role requires VPN access for daily work.
