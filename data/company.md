# Northwind Technologies — Company Handbook (Pre-Day-1 Edition)

## About Northwind

Northwind Technologies builds data and analytics tooling for mid-market logistics
companies. Founded in 2016, we're about 450 people today, headquartered in Austin
with a fully distributed engineering and data organization.

We're privately held and profitable. We don't run on a fundraising clock, and
roadmap decisions are made on a multi-year horizon rather than quarter to quarter.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make supply chain data usable by the people who actually move
freight, not just the analysts who model it.

Three values guide how we work:

- **Default to transparency** — internal documents, decisions, and roadmaps are
  open to employees unless there is a specific legal or privacy reason not to be.
- **Ship something real every two weeks** — we distrust work that cannot be
  demonstrated or tested.
- **Disagree, then commit** — debate design decisions openly, but once a decision
  is made, support it fully even if it was not your preference.

## Where we work

The Austin office is located at:

2400 Riverside Commons, Suite 400

The office is open Monday–Friday, 7 AM–8 PM, and badge access is required.

About 60% of the company works from Austin at least part-time. The rest of the
company is fully remote across the United States.

There is no company-wide return-to-office mandate. Teams set their own in-office
expectations, which are communicated by each employee's manager during the first
week.

Remote employees receive an annual travel budget to visit Austin or attend team
offsites.

## Org structure and leadership

Northwind has four main organizations:

- Engineering — led by the CTO
- Data & Analytics Product — led by the Chief Product Officer
- Go-to-Market — led by the CRO
- People & Operations — led by the COO

Each organization is divided into pods of approximately 6–10 people with a
single directly responsible lead.

The full leadership team's bios and organizational chart are posted on the
internal wiki under "About Northwind" once employees have system access on
day one.

Pre-day-1 access is intentionally limited to preboarding content.

## First-week onboarding

New employees spend their first week completing orientation, required security
and compliance training, meeting their manager and team, and setting up company
accounts and equipment.

Managers establish informal 30/60/90-day expectations during the first few weeks.

New hires are excluded from the formal performance-review cycle during their
first 90 days and instead receive informal check-ins with their manager.

## Code of conduct

Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind.

Concerns can be raised with:

- Your manager
- People Ops
- The anonymous ethics hotline linked in Workday

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a
violation subject to termination.

The full code of conduct is included in the day-one onboarding materials in
Workday.

## Diversity, equity, and inclusion

Northwind runs four employee resource groups that are open to all employees:

- Women in Data
- Northwind Pride
- Parents & Caregivers
- Veterans Network

Each group receives a discretionary budget and meets monthly.

ERG membership is optional and has no bearing on performance reviews,
compensation, or promotion. Participation is purely voluntary community-building.

## Communication tools

- Google Workspace — email and calendar

- Zoom — video calls

not before.

assistant or directly through the employee's recruiter or People Ops contact.

## IT and security basics

All company laptops ship with disk encryption and endpoint management
pre-installed. Employees do not need to configure these systems themselves.

Northwind requires two-factor authentication on every company account.

Employees must use the company-provided 1Password password manager for
work-related credentials.

Personal password reuse on company accounts is a policy violation.

VPN is required only for a small number of legacy internal tools. Most
day-to-day work happens over standard HTTPS with SSO.

## Benefits enrollment

Health, dental, and vision enrollment opens on an employee's start date and
remains open for 30 days.

Coverage is retroactive to the employee's first day regardless of when enrollment
is completed.

The 401(k) plan automatically enrolls eligible employees at 3% after 60 days
unless they opt out through Workday.

Employees do not need to complete benefits or 401(k) enrollment before their
start date.

## Expense policy

Expenses under $75 can be self-approved and submitted through Expensify.

Expenses above $75 require manager approval before the expense is incurred.

Standard reimbursable categories include:

- Client travel
- Conference attendance with manager pre-approval
- Home-office equipment beyond the initial stipend

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal and is subject to the
company-card policy limits posted in Expensify.

## Learning and development

Every employee receives a $1,500 annual learning budget for:

- Conferences
- Courses
- Books
- Certifications

Learning expenses under $200 do not require pre-approval.

Northwind also operates an internal mentorship program that employees can opt
into after their first 90 days.

The company covers tuition for job-relevant part-time degree or certificate
programs up to $5,000 per year, subject to a standard two-year retention
commitment.

## Performance reviews

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year:

- April
- October

New hires are excluded from the formal review cycle for their first 90 days.

Instead, new hires receive informal 30/60/90-day check-ins with their manager.

Compensation changes, when applicable, are tied to the formal review cycle
rather than being handled ad hoc.

## Remote work

Northwind supports fully remote employees across the United States.

There is no company-wide return-to-office mandate.

Individual teams may establish their own in-office expectations, which managers
communicate during the employee's first week.

Remote employees receive an annual travel budget for visits to Austin or company
offsites.

Questions about remote equipment or home-office stipends should be directed to
it-onboarding@example.com.
