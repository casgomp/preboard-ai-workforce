# Northwind Technologies — Company Handbook (Pre-Day-1 Edition)

## About Northwind
Northwind Technologies builds data and analytics tooling for mid-market logistics
companies. Founded in 2016, we're about 450 people today, with offices in Wolfsburg,
Berlin, and Munich and a fully distributed engineering and data org across those
three cities. We're privately held and profitable — we don't run on a fundraising
clock, and roadmap decisions are made on a multi-year horizon rather than quarter to
quarter.

## Mission and values
Our mission is to make supply chain data usable by the people who actually move
freight, not just the analysts who model it. Three values guide how we work: **default
to transparency** (internal docs, decisions, and roadmaps are open to all employees
unless there's a specific legal or privacy reason not to be); **ship something real
every two weeks** (we distrust work that can't be demoed); and **disagree, then
commit** (debate design decisions openly, but once a call is made, support it fully
even if it wasn't your preference).

## Where we work
The Wolfsburg office is our largest, with Berlin and Munich each running a full
engineering and product presence. All three are open Monday–Friday, 7 AM–8 PM, badge
access required. There's no company-wide return-to-office mandate — teams set their
own in-office expectations, communicated by your manager in your first week. Remote
and hybrid employees get an annual travel budget to visit another office or attend
team offsites.

## Org structure and leadership
Northwind has four main orgs: Engineering (led by the CTO), Data & Analytics Product
(led by the Chief Product Officer), Go-to-Market (led by the CRO), and People &
Operations (led by the COO). Each org is split into pods of 6–10 people with a single
directly responsible lead. The full leadership team's bios and an org chart are posted
on the internal wiki under "About Northwind" once you have system access on day one —
pre-day-1 access is intentionally limited to preboarding content only.

## Code of conduct
Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind. Concerns
can be raised to your manager, to People Operations, or — for anonymous reporting —
through the ethics hotline linked on the internal wiki. Retaliation against anyone who
raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation subject to termination. The full
code of conduct is part of your day-one onboarding packet.

## Diversity, equity, and inclusion
Northwind runs four employee resource groups open to all staff: Women in Data,
Northwind Pride, Parents & Caregivers, and Veterans Network. Each gets a discretionary
budget and meets monthly. ERG membership is opt-in and has no bearing on performance
reviews or promotion — participation is purely voluntary community-building.

## Communication tools
We use Slack for day-to-day communication, Google Workspace for email and calendar,
Notion for internal documentation, and Zoom for video calls. You'll receive Slack and
Google Workspace credentials on your first day, not before — pre-day-1 communication
goes through this preboarding assistant or directly with your People Operations
contact.

## IT and security basics
All company laptops ship with disk encryption and endpoint management pre-installed;
you won't need to configure anything yourself. Northwind requires two-factor
authentication on every company account and a password manager (1Password, provided)
for anything work-related — personal password reuse on company accounts is a policy
violation. VPN is required only for accessing a small number of legacy internal tools;
most day-to-day work happens over standard HTTPS with SSO.

## Expense policy
Expenses under €75 can be self-approved and submitted through Expensify; anything
above that needs your manager's sign-off before you spend, not after. Standard
reimbursable categories: client travel, conference attendance (with manager
pre-approval), and home-office equipment beyond the initial stipend. Alcohol is
reimbursable only as part of a client meal, capped at company card policy limits
posted in Expensify.

## Company holidays
Public holidays follow the calendar for your office's federal state (Wolfsburg and
Munich each observe a few state-specific days that Berlin doesn't, and vice versa).
The full calendar with exact dates for the current year is posted on the internal
wiki once your account is provisioned. Company-wide, we also close between Christmas
and New Year's.

## Learning and development
Every employee gets a €1,500/year learning budget (conferences, courses, books,
certifications) that doesn't require pre-approval under €200. Northwind also runs an
internal mentorship program you can opt into after your first 90 days, and covers
tuition for job-relevant part-time degree or certificate programs up to €5,000/year
with a standard 2-year retention commitment.

## Performance reviews
Formal performance reviews happen twice a year (April and October). New hires are
excluded from the review cycle for their first 90 days and instead get informal
30/60/90-day check-ins with their manager. Compensation changes, when they happen, are
tied to the review cycle, not ad hoc.

## Who to contact
For anything not covered here or in your offer packet: People Operations
(people-operations@dayone.demo) handles onboarding logistics, contracts, and pay
questions; Workplace IT (workplace-it@dayone.demo) handles equipment and account
provisioning; Facilities (facilities@dayone.demo) handles badges, desks, and office
access; and Learning & Onboarding (onboarding@dayone.demo) handles orientation and
training. Payroll typically can't process anything until your employee record exists
on or after your start date.
