# Offer Packet — David Klein, Product Manager

## Start date and first day
Your start date is confirmed in your offer letter, and the app shows the countdown
from there — this packet won't repeat the date since it can go stale. This role is
based in the Berlin office. Your manager, Nina Hoffmann, will meet you at reception
at 9:00 AM on your first day and walk you to orientation.

## Before your first day checklist
Bring a government-issued photo ID for right-to-work verification. No action is
needed on your reference check unless People Operations contacts you directly — it's
already in progress. Watch for your laptop, which should arrive about ten days before
your start date; contact workplace-it@dayone.demo if nothing has shown up three
business days before you start. There's nothing to complete for benefits before day
one.

## Equipment
IT ships a laptop to your home address about ten days before your start date. If
nothing has arrived three business days before you start, contact
workplace-it@dayone.demo. A desk in the Berlin office is assigned and ready.

## Benefits enrollment
Company benefits enrollment (supplemental health, meal allowance, and the company
pension top-up) opens on your start date and stays open for 30 days. Statutory health
insurance is arranged separately through your chosen provider before day one — your
offer letter has the details People Operations needs from you.

## Compensation and signing bonus
Your signing bonus pays out with your second paycheck, with a 12-month clawback if
you leave voluntarily before your first anniversary — same terms as every offer at
this level.

## Reference check
Your offer is contingent on a clean reference check, already in progress. People
Operations will contact you directly if anything is needed.

## Vacation days
Your statutory vacation entitlement is set out in your offer letter and is visible in
the HR system once your account is provisioned, typically by your second week.
